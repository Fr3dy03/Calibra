### Networks (verified against the official Quick Guide / JSON-RPC pages)

| Network | Chain ID | RPC | Explorer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mainnet | 677 | https://rpc.botchain.ai | https://scan.botchain.ai/ |
| Testnet | 968 | https://rpc.bohr.life | https://scan.bohr.life/ |

Native token: **BOT** (fixed supply 150M). BOT Chain is EVM-compatible ("nearly fully
Geth-compatible"; Parlia-derived consensus per docs). Additional notes:

- `eth_getLogs`: **disabled on the listed mainnet public endpoint** — use 3rd-party
  endpoints or WebSocket subscriptions for log-heavy indexing (relevant to our
  confirmation watcher; see INTEGRATION_LIMITATIONS.md).
- Blob transactions (EIP-4844) are supported — candidate channel for storing the raw
  DCC digest bundle cheaply (flagged as future work, not v1 scope).
- Faucet: BOT Chain testnet faucet (linked from the Quick Guide). Mainnet BOT via DEX.
